# Changelog

Version history for the Claude Boot Camp curriculum.

## v0.1 — 2026-09-11

Initial structure. Six sections outlined, card method and verification discipline established. No cards yet.

## v0.2 — 2026-09-11

Planning decisions folded in from the seminar session: knowledge-primitive framing
(cards are the product, outputs are renderings), tangent-following working style,
the two-session role split, the do-not-recreate-Anthropic-Academy principle
(lectures may be pointers, labs stay original), learning inputs, and framing.md
for the eventual front matter.

## v0.3 — 2026-09-11

First six cards filed (the planning session itself, carded). Removed framing.md —
that content is now card 005. Added a dictation proper-noun glossary to CLAUDE.md.

## v0.4 — 2026-09-11

Card schema: added `preference` and `decision` types and `n/a` verification, so
personal working style is never presented as verified fact. Reclassified the first
six cards; three are now preferences, one a decision.

## v0.5 — 2026-09-11

Cards 007-010 from the Claude Projects seminar session: Projects mechanics
(verified against official docs), Projects vs CLAUDE.md, the local-tooling

## v0.6 — 2026-09-11

Cards 011-012: passive context vs active retrieval as a unifying lens, and the
distribution tradeoff (curated subset in a Project vs full corpus over MCP).
